## Refund exposure

Derived refund amount is approximately **₹38.17L**. Damaged Product contributes approximately **₹8.03L**, the largest refund amount among individual standardized return reasons.

The top three return reasons contribute approximately **₹19.84L**, or about **52.0%** of total derived refunds.

## Interpretation guardrails

- High return volume identifies priority areas but does not prove causality.
- Channel concentration does not prove that a channel causes higher returns.
- Discount/return relationships should be tested rather than assumed.
- Operational root causes require additional evidence such as complaint text, packaging, carrier, seller and controlled-test data.
